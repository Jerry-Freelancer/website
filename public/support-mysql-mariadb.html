MySQL/MariaDB Support and Services

This page is based on the service scope described by Percona's MySQL support and services offering, adapted into concise wording for your site.

Source reference: percona.com/mysql/support-and-services.

Support

24x7 on-demand expert help for production incidents, slow query troubleshooting, security concerns, configuration validation, and backup/recovery reliability.

Managed Services

Proactive monitoring, alerting, incident response, reporting, and operational ownership to reduce outages and free your team for strategic work.

Consulting

Architecture design, migrations, upgrades, scaling strategies, performance tuning, and security assessments for complex MySQL and MariaDB environments.

Training

Instructor-led training tracks for DBAs and developers, including replication, high availability, backup/recovery, indexing, and troubleshooting practices.

Why teams choose this model

  • Faster incident resolution with experienced responders.
  • Lower operational risk through standardized processes and regular health checks.
  • Flexible support across cloud, on-prem, and hybrid infrastructure.
